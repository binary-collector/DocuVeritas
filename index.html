Capabilities

Comprehensive Document & Identity Screening

Our screening engine combines multiple analytical layers to detect document tampering, synthetic identities, and biometric anomalies in real time.

🔬

Forensic Document Analysis

Deep pixel-level examination of fonts, microprinting, security threads, watermarks, and substrate composition to detect physical forgeries.

🧬

MRZ & Barcode Validation

Automated parsing and checksum verification of Machine-Readable Zones (ICAO 9303), PDF417 barcodes, and QR codes against official standards.

🤖

AI Fraud Detection

Forensic computer vision heuristics and anomaly models detect digital splicing, photo substitution, and font irregularities invisible to the naked eye.

👤

Facial Biometric Matching

Compare identity document portraits to live captures with biometric facial landmark analysis, liveness verification, and GAN/deepfake artifact detection.

🔗

Cross-Reference Intelligence

Evaluates identity consistency against synthetic identity patterns, disposable email networks, VoIP lines, and known fraud indicators.

📊

Risk Scoring & Analytics

Comprehensive risk scores with explainable AI — every flag comes with clear reasoning and supporting evidence for audit trails.

Process

How DocuVeritas Works

From document upload to verified result in under 5 seconds — our four-stage pipeline ensures thorough yet rapid screening.

1

Upload / Capture

Upload a document image or capture it via webcam. Our pre-processor corrects skew, lighting, and resolution automatically.

2

Data Extraction

OCR and MRZ parsers extract all text fields, barcodes, and embedded data. The document type and issuing country are auto-classified.

3

Multi-Layer Analysis

AI models run 30+ forensic checks: font analysis, security feature detection, photo integrity, cross-field validation, and database lookups.

4

Verdict & Report

A confidence score and detailed report are generated with pass/fail per check, flagged anomalies, and recommended actions.
